## 2026-09-15 — Arrive early, leave late

> Arrive 10 minutes earlier than the boss, leave 10 minutes after the boss leaves.

---

## 2026-08-30 — Master the games or be mastered

> Whether you're aware of it or not, there are games currently running your life right now. You can either master those games, or those games will master you and steal away your time, money and energy. Choice is yours.
>
> You'll know you've mastered game theory when people say "you got lucky."

---

## 2026-08-30 — Find the power move

> Find the move that makes the whole game easier, instead of just grinding through it. Whatever game you're playing has one of these power moves. Your job is to find out what it is and exploit it. Become obsessed with that move.

---

## 2026-08-30 — Ask the winners if it was worth it

> Before playing any game, study the people who've already won — then, if you can, ask them: "Was winning this game worth it? Did it make you happy?"

---

## 2026-08-30 — Reverse-engineer the winners

> Treat your life like a bizarre video game. Remember: if someone has won the game you want to play before, you can reverse-engineer most of what they did and produce similar results.

---

## 2026-08-30 — Winning the wrong game

> Even if you win the rat race, you're still a rat. Effort spent inside a rigged game gets absorbed by the game.
>
> You can be the best bidder in the dollar auction. Being the best bidder is exactly how you lose $40. What protects your money isn't doing the same thing everyone else does, only harder. It's a completely different skill: looking at a game, seeing how it works before you're pulled in, and walking away from it.

---

## 2026-08-23 — Just existing

> You work that job, you pay your rent, you come home for Christmas. That's just existing.

---

## 2026-08-22 — If it was easy

> If it was easy, everyone would do it.

---

## 2026-08-22 — Aim higher

> Aim higher: higher than other people think you should. Higher than you think you can.
>
> You don't have to know exactly how everything will work out. But you do have to stop deciding in advance what you're not capable of.
