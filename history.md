## How to add a row

1. Add the link to its normal page first — blogs and posts to [articles.md](articles.md), videos to [youtube.md](youtube.md), repositories to [repos.md](repos.md), everything else to [links.md](links.md).
2. Append one row at the **bottom** of the table above, with the next number.
3. Bump the count in the banner and update _Last added_.

**Type** — `Blog`, `Article`, `Video`, `Repo`, `Doc`, `Paper`, `Tool`
**Topic** — `AI / LLM`, `GPU / Performance`, `Digital Verification`, `Computer Architecture`, `Misc`
**Date** — `YYYY-MM-DD`
